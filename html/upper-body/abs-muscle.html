ABS

CABLE CRUNCHES

Cable crunches strengthen the abdominal muscles by adding resistance to a controlled forward crunch. They are useful for building core strength while keeping the movement focused and controlled.

LEG RAISE

Leg raises target the lower abdominal muscles by lifting the legs with control. The exercise also challenges the hip flexors and encourages steady core stability.

RUSSIAN TWIST

Russian twists work the abdominal muscles and obliques through controlled torso rotation. Keeping the chest lifted helps maintain tension throughout the movement.
