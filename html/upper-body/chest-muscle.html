CHEST

BENCH PRESS

The bench press builds the chest, shoulders, and triceps through a controlled pushing motion. It is a compound exercise that helps develop upper-body pressing strength.

CABLE FLY

Cable flyes isolate the chest by bringing the hands together against steady cable resistance. The exercise encourages a controlled stretch and squeeze through the chest.

MACHINE

The chest machine trains the chest muscles with a stable, guided pressing movement. Its fixed path can help beginners focus on form and muscle control.
