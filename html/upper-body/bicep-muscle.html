BICEP

BICEP CURL

Bicep curls isolate the biceps by bending the elbows to lift the weight with control. Keeping the upper arms still helps the biceps do most of the work.

CONCENTRATION CURL

Concentration curls focus on one biceps muscle at a time and encourage a strict range of motion. The supported position makes it easier to avoid swinging the weight.

HAMMER CURL

Hammer curls train the biceps and forearms while using a neutral hand position. They can also help build grip strength through the controlled lifting motion.
