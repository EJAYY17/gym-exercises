HAMSTRING

DEADLIFT

Deadlifts train the hamstrings, glutes, and back through a strong hip-hinge movement. Keeping the spine braced helps build posterior-chain strength safely.

ROMANIAN DEADLIFT

Romanian deadlifts emphasize the hamstrings by lowering the weight with a controlled hip hinge. The exercise also strengthens the glutes while improving posterior-chain control.
