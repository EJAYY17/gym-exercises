CALVES

SEATED CALF RAISE

Seated calf raises target the soleus muscle by raising the heels while the knees remain bent. Using a controlled tempo helps build strength through the full range of motion.

STANDING CALF RAISE

Standing calf raises emphasize the gastrocnemius by lifting the heels while the legs stay straight. They can improve lower-leg strength and ankle stability when performed with control.
