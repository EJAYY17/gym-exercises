QUADRICEPS

WEIGHTED SQUAT

Weighted squats strengthen the quadriceps, glutes, and core through a controlled lowering and standing motion. They are a foundational lower-body exercise for building strength and stability.

LEG EXTENSION

Leg extensions isolate the quadriceps by straightening the knees against machine resistance. A slow return keeps the muscles working throughout the exercise.

DUMBBELL LUNGES

Dumbbell lunges train the quadriceps and glutes one leg at a time while challenging balance. They also develop coordination and strength through a functional stepping pattern.
